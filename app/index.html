Aggregate book — what you trade against BUYSELLyour curve @ size AGGREGATEYOU
Each maker posts its own curve. The book you trade is the envelope — best ask and best bid at every strike — so the aggregate is always at least as tight as any single maker. The curve is not a line — each maker is an AMM, so taking size walks its own curve continuously and the book is a cloud: brightness is the liquidity still untouched at that price, full at the touch and fading to nothing where the book is exhausted, so how far the cloud reaches IS the depth. BUY and SELL are separate tabs; you only ever see one side. The panel below is the landing map — the aggregation is nontrivial, so the honest object is a surface landed(k,Q), not a single curve: pick a strike and a size and it shows where that trade lands you relative to the best quote. In an RFQ makers are free to differ — there is no shared resting book to cross. Where the envelope inverts, that is a genuine arbitrage, and arbitrageurs taking it are what discipline an outlier maker. Drag maker divergence to see it open and close.
