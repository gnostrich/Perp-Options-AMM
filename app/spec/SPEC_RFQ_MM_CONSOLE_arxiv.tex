\begin{abstract}
We specify a request-for-quote (RFQ) market-maker console for perpetual options in which a
maker's quote at \emph{every} strike is a single closed-form curve: four parameters
$(\Sbar,a,\gamma,\kappa)$ of a Burr-II (Burr Type XII in the moneyness modulus) wing kernel,
plus a half-spread $h$. There is no order ladder: any strike is priced on demand, requoting is
four parameters rather than $N$ orders, and the two curve sets (bid and ask) make the spread an
irreversible revenue band. Makers differ only in depth and spread while sharing the price
\emph{level} --- a design forced by a proved theorem (heterogeneous per-maker steepness generates
a strict volatility smile that no single power-law lens can represent). The tradeable book is the
envelope of maker curves; RFQ fills walk makers cheapest-first, each paid at its own posted
spread; settlement nets in perpetual units and crosses one common units$\to$cash doorway, which
is exactly the arbitrage-free class. We give the closed form, the quoting economics
(vol-indexed spreads against gamma bleed), the aggregation and fill rules, the user-interface
contract, worked numbers re-derived from the shipped code, and the complete formal corpus
backing the design: five Lean~4 packages, 130 theorems, all kernel-checked locally with axiom
set $\{\mathsf{propext},\mathsf{Classical.choice},\mathsf{Quot.sound}\}$. Open gaps are stated
rather than hidden.
\end{abstract}

\section{The pricing kernel}\label{sec:kernel}

Let $S$ be spot, $K$ a strike, and $k = K/S - 1$ the linear moneyness (calls are out of the money
for $k\ge 0$, puts for $k\le 0$; the zero-strike edge is $k=-1$). Fix parameters
$\Sbar>0$ (mean strike scale), $a>0$ (Burr shape), $\gamma>0$ (tail weight), $\kappa\in(-1,1)$
(skew). Write $B = \Gamma(\tfrac1a)\Gamma(\tfrac\gamma a)/\Gamma(\tfrac1a+\tfrac\gamma a)
= \mathrm{B}(\tfrac1a,\tfrac\gamma a)$ and let $\ib_u(\cdot,\cdot)$ be the regularized
incomplete beta function.

\paragraph{Wing scales and kernel.}
\begin{equation}
s_R = \Sbar(1+\kappa), \qquad s_L = \Sbar(1-\kappa), \qquad
\Delta_s(v) = \Bigl(1 + (v/s)^a\Bigr)^{-\frac{\gamma+1}{a}} .
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Wing value (tail integral), pedestal, masses.}
\begin{equation}
T_s(m) = \frac{s}{a}\,B\,\Bigl(1 - \ib_{u}\bigl(\tfrac1a,\tfrac\gamma a\bigr)\Bigr),
\quad u = \frac{m^a}{m^a + s^a}, \qquad
G_1 = \Delta_{s_L}(1), \quad I_1 = T_{s_L}(1),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
W_R = \frac{s_R B}{a}, \qquad
W_L = \frac{s_L B/a - I_1 - G_1}{1 - G_1} .
\end{equation}

\paragraph{The peg (derived, not chosen).}
\begin{equation}
q_R = \frac{W_L}{W_R + W_L}, \qquad q_L = 1 - q_R
\end{equation}
is the \emph{unique} split making the two wings meet at the money
(Lean: \texttt{atm\_wings\_meet}); at-the-money put--call parity \emph{is} the peg.

\paragraph{Wings and option values.}
\begin{align}
A_R(k) &= q_R\,T_{s_R}(|k|), \\
A_L(k) &= \begin{cases}
\dfrac{q_L}{1-G_1}\Bigl(T_{s_L}(|k|) - I_1 - G_1\,(1-|k|)\Bigr), & |k| < 1,\\[4pt]
0, & |k| \ge 1,
\end{cases}\\
\mathrm{CALL}(k) &= \begin{cases} A_R(k), & k \ge 0\\ -k + A_L(k), & k<0\end{cases}
\qquad
\mathrm{PUT}(k) = \begin{cases} A_L(k), & k \le 0\\ \;\,k + A_R(k), & k>0.\end{cases}
\end{align}
Out of the money the value is the wing; in the money it is intrinsic plus the \emph{mirror}
wing, so
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;\mathrm{CALL}(k) - \mathrm{PUT}(k) = -k \quad\text{at every strike}\;}
\tag{parity}
\end{equation}
(Lean: \texttt{burr2\_parity}; away from $k=0$ it is hypothesis-free branch bookkeeping,
\texttt{burr2\_parity\_of\_ne}). The left wing's closed form already vanishes at the zero-strike
edge (\texttt{AL\_glue}), giving the exact anchors $\mathrm{CALL}(-1)=1$ (the whole coin) and
$\mathrm{PUT}(-1)=0$. The at-the-money mark is $\mathrm{ATM} = q_R W_R$. Sign and order
--- all values nonnegative, $\mathrm{CALL}$ antitone and Lipschitz, $\mathrm{PUT}$ monotone ---
hold conditionally on a two-sided Riemann-sandwich interface for the incomplete beta
(\texttt{TailRep}, proved non-vacuous by an explicit witness); see \S\ref{sec:gaps}.

\section{Quoting: two curve sets and vol-indexed spreads}\label{sec:quoting}

\paragraph{The curve is the quote.} A maker posts $(\Sbar,a,\gamma,\kappa)$ and a half-spread
$h$ (bps). At any strike, on demand,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ask}(k) = V(k)\,\bigl(1+\tfrac{h}{10^4}\bigr), \qquad
\mathrm{bid}(k) = V(k)\,\bigl(1-\tfrac{h}{10^4}\bigr),
\end{equation}
with $V \in \{\mathrm{CALL},\mathrm{PUT}\}$. There is no ladder to post, cancel, or replace;
requoting is four parameters, not $N$ orders. The band between the two curve sets is the
maker's revenue and is \emph{irreversible} (unlike constant-product-AMM slippage, which a
round trip refunds). Execution is priced on the \emph{continuous (integral) convention}: impact
is a price path, not maker revenue --- the endpoint-price shortcut that fabricated ``slippage
revenue'' was identified and removed before this app was built (operator rulings, transcript
entries 539--540).

\paragraph{Gamma bleed and the fair spread.} With cash value
$C_{\text{cash}}(S,K) = \mathrm{CALL}(K/S-1)\cdot S$, the console measures a per-dollar-notional
cash curvature
\begin{equation}
\tilde G = \operatorname*{mean}_{K/S \in \{0.85,0.95,1,1.05,1.15\}}
\Bigl|\,S\,\partial^2_S C_{\text{cash}}(S,K)\Bigr|,
\qquad
\text{bleed} = \tfrac12\,\tilde G\,\mathrm{RV}^2 \;\;[\text{notional}/\text{yr}],
\end{equation}
and the fair half-spread and quote
\begin{equation}
h_{\text{fair}} = \frac{\text{bleed}}{\text{turnover}\cdot 365}\times 10^4\ \text{bps},
\qquad
h_{\text{eff}} =
\begin{cases}
h_{\text{fair}}\cdot(1+25\%) & \text{vol-indexed mode},\\
\text{fee}\cdot 10^4/2 & \text{fixed-spread mode}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
Fees, bleed and returns per year on quoted notional $N_\$$ (margin $\times$ 10 pool leverage,
both wings) are
\begin{equation}
\text{fees} = \tfrac{h_{\text{eff}}}{10^4}\,\text{turn}\cdot 365\cdot N_\$,\quad
\text{bleed}_\$ = \text{bleed}\cdot N_\$,\quad
\text{APR}_{\text{base}} = \tfrac{\text{fees}-\text{bleed}_\$}{N_\$},\quad
\text{APR}_{\text{lev}} = \tfrac{\text{fees}-\text{bleed}_\$}{\text{equity}}.
\end{equation}
Capacity is a \emph{continuous} density $w(k)\propto e^{-8\lambda k^2}$ normalized to the wing
notional --- the ribbon in the chart, not rungs. The economics layer is a model with no Lean
backing (\S\ref{sec:gaps}).

\section{Aggregation, the RFQ, and settlement}\label{sec:agg}

\paragraph{Common level, private transport.} All makers share the level parameters
$(\Sbar,a,\gamma,\kappa)$; each maker owns only its depth $\lambda_i$ and half-spread $h_i$.
This is not a stylistic choice: \texttt{mixture\_not\_single\_lens} (\S\ref{sec:lean}) proves
that mixing distinct per-maker steepnesses produces a strictly log-convex (smiled) aggregate
that \emph{no} single power-law lens represents, and build~11's own test reproduced the failure
empirically --- per-maker levels crossed the book (best bid $0.15482 >$ best ask $0.12015$,
spread $-1{,}261$ bps). The shipped design is ``transport, not level,'' with an honest
\textsf{CROSSED --- arb} detector that cannot fire under a common level and positive spreads.

\paragraph{Depth aggregation (harmonic).}
\begin{equation}
\frac{1}{\lambda_{\text{agg}}} = \sum_i \frac{1}{\lambda_i}, \qquad
\text{share}_i = \frac{1/\lambda_i}{\sum_j 1/\lambda_j}
\end{equation}
(Lean: \texttt{harmonic\_law}, \texttt{shares\_sum\_one}, \texttt{share\_eq\_agg\_ratio}; the
parallel-addition law itself is \emph{earned} in the base layer from walking a stacked ladder
to a common marginal price, \texttt{walk\_equiv}/\texttt{walk\_cost\_equiv}, interior case).

\paragraph{Envelope book and RFQ fill.}
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ask}(k) = \min_i V_i(k)\bigl(1+\tfrac{h_i}{10^4}\bigr), \qquad
\mathrm{bid}(k) = \max_i V_i(k)\bigl(1-\tfrac{h_i}{10^4}\bigr),
\end{equation}
so the aggregate is always at least as tight as any single maker (the ``improvement vs.\ single
maker'' panel quantifies what competition buys). An RFQ of size $Q$ walks makers
cheapest-first, fills each up to $\text{share}_i \times \text{pool}$, and pays each maker
\emph{at its own posted spread} (pay-your-own-quote, not uniform-price; Lean:
\texttt{apportionment\_conserves}).

\paragraph{Settlement doorway.} Legs net in perpetual \emph{units}; cash appears only through
one common conversion at the exit. This is forced, not convenient:
\texttt{doorway\_arbfree\_iff\_common} proves a book netting to zero units pays zero cash
\emph{iff} the units$\to$cash factor is common across strikes, and any per-strike factor admits
unbounded arbitrage (witness: long $1/V_i$ at strike $i$, short $1/V_j$ at strike $j$ --- zero
net units, cash $F_i - F_j$, scalable). \texttt{exit\_timing\_irrelevant}: a netted-flat book
settles to zero for every closing equity and leverage.

\paragraph{Trade bands and portfolio.} A band sells one wing and buys the other with the
proceeds (per-leg fee and slippage); \emph{only $\kappa$ moves} --- level and shape stay put ---
and every strike re-prices at once. The portfolio view keeps perps and perp-options in one
account equity (account-level liquidation, $50\times$ cap with headroom), carves option-backing
notional out of the perps tab, and reads net option delta back to a perp hedge with zero
residual. The $\kappa$ dynamics and the exposure readback have \emph{no} Lean backing
(\S\ref{sec:gaps}).

\section{The formal corpus}\label{sec:lean}

Five Lean~4 packages, 130 theorems. Uniformly: no
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{admit}/\texttt{axiom}/\texttt{native\_decide}/\texttt{opaque}/%
\texttt{unsafe}; every theorem's \texttt{\#print axioms} $=$
$\{\mathsf{propext},\mathsf{Classical.choice},\mathsf{Quot.sound}\}$; statements byte-identical
to what was submitted (prover-side edits are proof bodies and private helpers only); all
locally kernel-verified in Lean 4.28.0 (Mathlib \texttt{.olean}s from the CI cache --- stated,
not hidden). Label-flip from \emph{trusted-from-prover} to \emph{verified} is on record as a
recommendation; the call is the operator's.

\subsection{\texttt{BURR2\_CORE} --- 42 theorems (Aristotle run \texttt{26958f2b})}
Theorems about the \emph{exact} production formulas of \S\ref{sec:kernel}, conditional on
nothing but four interface facts about the incomplete beta (carried as a structure field, not an
axiom). \S1 sheet correspondence and positivity (\texttt{uArg\_eq\_sheet},
\texttt{tail\_zero}, \texttt{G1\_pos}, \texttt{G1\_lt\_one}, \dots); \S2 parity
(\texttt{burr2\_parity\_of\_ne} hypothesis-free off-ATM; \texttt{atm\_wings\_meet}: the peg is
\emph{derived} as the unique wing-meeting split; \texttt{burr2\_parity}); \S3 zero-strike
anchor (\texttt{AL\_glue}, $\mathrm{CALL}(-1)=1$, $\mathrm{PUT}(-1)=0$); \S4 sign and order
(19 theorems: kernel in $(0,1]$ decreasing, wings nonnegative, \texttt{CALL\_antitone},
\texttt{CALL\_lipschitz}, \texttt{PUT\_monotone} --- conditional on \texttt{TailRep}); \S5
non-vacuity witness; \S6 apportionment (\texttt{harmonic\_law}, \texttt{shares\_sum\_one},
\texttt{share\_eq\_agg\_ratio}, \texttt{share\_nonneg}, \texttt{apportionment\_conserves}).
Two substantive corrections the proofs forced on the brief: at-the-money parity \emph{is} the
peg (the $1.39\times10^{-17}$ and $2.44\times10^{-13}$ residuals are one phenomenon, not two),
and $q_R = W_L/(W_R{+}W_L)$ is derived, not chosen.

\subsection{\texttt{BURR2\_MIXTURE} --- 13 theorems (run \texttt{e95f6377})}
Burr-II is \emph{not} closed under maker mixture
(\texttt{burr2\_not\_closed\_under\_mixture}, and the \S5 stretch version for scale mixing via
a two-atom moment/Cauchy--Schwarz argument), \emph{but} the exact identity
\begin{equation}
\tfrac12\bigl(\Delta_{\gamma_1} + \Delta_{\gamma_2}\bigr)
= \Delta_{\bar\gamma}\cdot\cosh(\delta\,L_c)
\end{equation}
bounds the mixture as the same shape times $1+O(\delta^2)$ --- \emph{second} order in the
parameter gap, versus the single-lens wall of \S\ref{sec:agg} which is first-order structural.
The kernel change is what buys that mildness.

\subsection{\texttt{LOOP\_LINK\_A} (pricing) --- 9 theorems (project \texttt{20fa5993}, task \texttt{b1e0c962})}
The pricing$\leftrightarrow$aggregation bridge. \texttt{engine\_call\_midconvex} ($g\ge1$, via
a global supporting line) discharges the standing \textsf{MidConvex} hypothesis for the v28
engine curve; \texttt{atm\_kink\_bound} in the stronger form
$(g{+}1)\bigl(\tfrac{g+1}{g}\bigr)^{g} \ge 2g+1$ for all $g>0$;
\texttt{engine\_book\_arb\_free} / \texttt{engine\_book\_parity}: a book of makers each with
its own exponent $g_i$ is butterfly-arbitrage-free and parity-anchored. And the load-bearing
negative half: \texttt{engine\_log\_affine} $+$ \texttt{mixture\_strict\_log\_convex} $\implies$
\texttt{mixture\_not\_single\_lens} --- \emph{heterogeneous maker steepness generates a strict
smile that no single power-law lens can represent}. This proved obstruction is why the console's
makers share the level (\S\ref{sec:agg}).

\subsection{\texttt{LOOP\_LINK\_B} (settlement) --- 12 theorems (project \texttt{ce3c2190}, task \texttt{aa5f90f8})}
Netting in units is linear and well-defined (\texttt{units\_add}, \texttt{units\_smul},
\texttt{cashOne\_add}); the common doorway neither creates value nor flips signs
(\texttt{cashOne\_zero\_iff}, \texttt{cashOne\_pos\_iff}); it cannot be timed
(\texttt{exit\_timing\_irrelevant}); and the pair
\texttt{common\_doorway\_necessary} / \texttt{per\_strike\_doorway\_unbounded\_arb} yields
\texttt{doorway\_arbfree\_iff\_common}: one common units$\to$cash factor is \emph{exactly} the
arbitrage-free class. ``Dollars enter only at the very end'' is a theorem.

\subsection{\texttt{sims/v3-maps-lean} (base layer) --- 54 theorems}
The book/aggregation substrate (11 \texttt{BOOK} $+$ 25 \texttt{MAP} $+$ 18 \texttt{BASIS}):
\texttt{agg\_parity} (weights summing to one), \texttt{agg\_midconvex} (nonnegative weights;
fixed-across-strikes enforced structurally), \texttt{book\_arb\_free} (butterfly leg),
\texttt{level\_convex\_iff\_zero\_spread\_arbfree} (a genuine iff, the strongest statement in
the file), \texttt{share\_strike\_invariant}, \texttt{beta\_transport\_parallel} with the
parallel-addition law earned by \texttt{walk\_equiv}/\texttt{walk\_cost\_equiv} (interior
case), and the exact vega dichotomy \texttt{shares\_fixed\_of\_common\_transport} /
\texttt{common\_transport\_is\_necessary}.

\section{Open gaps (stated, not hidden)}\label{sec:gaps}

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
\item \textbf{The Burr-II MidConvex bridge is not proved.} The book-layer arbitrage-freeness
chain attaches to the v28 engine power-law curve (\texttt{engine\_call\_midconvex}) and only
\emph{hypothesis-conditionally} to the Burr-II curve this console actually quotes. Named the
next Lean obligation.
\item \texttt{BURR2\_CORE} \S4 sign/order results are conditional on \texttt{TailRep}; the
non-vacuity witness is a toy ($a=\gamma=1$, $\ib=\mathrm{id}$), not the real incomplete beta.
\item The \texttt{BURR2\_MIXTURE} bound is on the midpoint member, near-the-money in scope
(residuals of $0.02$--$0.12\%$ are near-money; $745\%$ excess at $\gamma$ $0.4$ vs $3.0$);
joint non-closure over all four parameters is unproved.
\item $\kappa$ (skew) dynamics --- the Trade Bands mechanic --- are entirely unformalised.
\item \textbf{Mislabel risk (base layer):} the exposure readback (\texttt{Exposure}) is cited
in \texttt{v3-maps-lean}'s README and the loop map but is \emph{absent} from the Lean files
(re-verified 2026-08-18); the Portfolio hedge-readback panel has zero Lean backing.
\item ``Arb-free'' means \emph{butterfly} only: no unconditional vertical leg, no price bounds
($C\ge\text{intrinsic}$, $C\le S$); and there is no individual-rationality/adverse-selection
theorem for makers under the mixed book (the refraction finding).
\item The economics layer (bleed, $h_{\text{fair}}$, APR, viability) is a model with demo
constants and no Lean; the fixed-mode inversion is unreachable at the default fee (see
\S\ref{sec:numbers}). Realtime maker tuning converts a passive-LP product into an active-MM
venue --- adverse selection, correlated withdrawal, and herding are new, unmodelled risks
(operator transcript entry 541).
\item The console is not wired to the production engine; nothing here changes
\texttt{engine/} or its gates.
\end{enumerate}
