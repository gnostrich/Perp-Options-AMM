E — the curve IS the cloudno line: ask depth spreads UP, bid depth spreads DOWN, brightness = size you can get at that priceentry 559
Each maker is an AMM, so filling size walks its own curve continuously — there is no block of stock at one price and therefore no rung. At every strike, and every price, we ask: how much can I get here? qᵢ(p) = clamp( (p − askᵢ) · 0.01·N / (λᵢ·ATMᵢ), 0, shareᵢ·N ) and Q(k,p) = Σᵢ qᵢ(p). Brightness is the marginal depth ∂Q/∂p, so the cloud ends where the book is exhausted rather than flooding upward. Teal spreads upward from the best ask (buy deeper ⇒ pay more); red spreads downward from the best bid (sell deeper ⇒ receive less). The dark seam between them is the spread. A thin cloud is a thin book; a fat cloud is a deep one — the shape you see is the liquidity distribution, not a summary of it.
