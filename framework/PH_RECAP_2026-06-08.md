## 1. PH formulation recap (core math)

Temporal is modelled as a **discrete-time port-Hamiltonian (PH) system** `(H, J, R, ports)` with the
energy balance

```
H(next) = H(now) + supplied(ports) − dissipated,    dissipated ≥ 0.
```

The abstract core is `Temporal.PassiveSystem` (`RequestProject/Temporal.lean §1`), curve-agnostic and
**proved-in-prompt** (fully, no sorry; axiom set is the standard propext/Classical.choice/Quot.sound).
A real round-trip on it would land at `trusted-from-prover` (no PENDING-LEAN; Aristotle's server compile
is the build). Each PH ingredient is then instantiated onto the GH geometry.

### H — storage / value function (PH-1)
`H = equity = intrinsic(price) + support`, where
`intrinsic C O p := C.poolValue p − O p` and `poolValue p = sInf_x { p·x + y(x) }` is the lower
envelope of the reserve frontier (`Seam.lean`). `O` is the convex obligation the pool owes; `support`
is accrued external port (funding/hedge). The carry **P = Ny/Nx** anchors the price coordinate
`u = log price − log P`. The GH frontier gives **value ∝ S^(−γ), γ>1**.
- Core fact: `intrinsic_concaveOn` — intrinsic reserve value is **concave in price for every valid
  AMMCurve** (short-gamma is universal). `proved-in-prompt`.
- **GH instance still open** — `H` is wired abstractly; the GH `AMMCurve` instance (4 gate fields) is
  the GH gate-discharge, `open`. So PH-1 is **scaffolded**, not discharged for GH.

### J — skew-symmetric interconnection / lossless routing (PH-2)
A pure trade routes power between X- and Y-ports without creating/destroying storage: `J` is
skew-symmetric ⇔ the trade map conserves the curve invariant. Constant-product shadow is
`Barrier.boost δ = (e^δ·X, e^(−δ)·Y)`, `Phi = X·Y`; `boost_preserves_Phi`, `boost_zero`, `boost_add`
(one-parameter group) are **proved-in-prompt**.
- **GH invariant open.** For GH the conserved quantity is **not** `X·Y`; the GH trade is the
  quantile-remap `tradeUpdate` (price-coordinate, factor `e^(−ghMu)`). The Lean `boost` must be the
  *same* map, not a re-derived one. **Stop-and-flag** if the GH `tradeUpdate` conserves no clean
  invariant (→ economic-object finding for the operator).

### R ⪰ 0 — dissipation / arb-LVR (PH-3, = B3)
The resistive structure is PSD: per-tick dissipation `arbLeak s ≥ 0` (LVR net of fees). Carried as the
**field** `arb_nonneg : ∀ s, 0 ≤ arbLeak s` in `TemporalAMM`/`CurvePool` (a hypothesis FIELD, not an
axiom decl — allowed). `scaffolded`. The PSD *upgrade* (express `arbLeak` as `vᵀRv`, `R⪰0` in the
slope-deviation variable, prove nonnegativity from R) is `open` — engine-grounded B3.

### Ports — funding + trade
`supplied = portFlow` (premium + oracle-drive + hedge + LP in/out); `dissipated = arbLeak`. The
funding potential `Hwell S = S − log S` is **bounded below by 1** (`Hwell_bddBelow`, via `log x ≤ x−1`)
— PH-7, `proved-in-prompt`. This is the model funding well, the port that must cover the convex deficit.

### Passivity / no-free-lunch (PH-4a)
`passivity`: `H(run s n) ≤ H s + cumSupplied s n` — storage cannot be manufactured. `closed_cycle`
(`no_free_lunch`): a closed cycle with no net supply dissipates nothing and supplies exactly zero — no
pumping loop. Both **proved-in-prompt** (curve-agnostic; fire on any `CurvePool` via the reduction).

### Reserves-have-no-floor / "convexity must be funded" (PH-4b)
The load-bearing honest result. A strictly convex obligation has **no** reserve lower bound:
`reserves_have_no_floor` proves `¬ BddBelow (intrinsic (cpmm k) (p↦p²) '' Ioi 0)` (since
`poolValue p ≤ p+k` while `O = p²` grows, intrinsic → −∞). **proved-in-prompt for cpmm/O=p²; GH
analogue open.** PH reading: the reserve geometry is **not intrinsically coercive against a convex
claim — the floor is a PORT property, not a storage property.** This makes the funding port
**NECESSARY, not sufficient**; sufficiency is **B1** (undischarged solvency prize).
- **Pin (do not conflate):** `reserves_have_no_floor` = `¬ BddBelow ((intrinsic C O)'' dom)` for a
  fixed strictly-convex `O`. This is **NOT** the negation of the `coercive` gate field (which is about
  `poolValue` ALONE, no `O`, and DOES hold on GH's bounded reserves). Stating them as the same thing is
  the error to avoid. The §1 `H_floor`/`solvent` field is a *different* (assumed) floor = B1.

### PH-5 / PH-6 / PH-7 (recap, status in §2)
- **PH-5** — C¹ of H at the smooth-pasting boundary `S* = Kγ/(γ+1)` (`sNorm* = θ·((γ+1)/γ)^γ`,

## 3. What we built that feeds theory but ISN'T covered (the generative half)

Build-derived theoretical content not yet captured by PH-1…PH-7 or the C/B queue. Each: claim in
core-math terms, why it matters, and a queue/escalate call.

### (a) Strike-registration as a coordinate/gauge-invariance theorem — NEW, queue-worthy
**Claim.** Registering the strike in the curve's carry coordinate, `θ = sNorm(K) = (oracle₀/S)^γ`
(via `getSNorm∘arbitrageToOracle`, NOT a finite difference), makes the OTM→ITM crossover land at the
**dollar strike K for all γ**, whereas the price-ratio registration `θ = K/oracle` (∝ S⁻¹) drifts the
crossover to `oracle₀²/K` for γ>1 (they agree only at γ=1). Equivalently: the crossover location is a
**coordinate-invariant** of the instrument iff the strike is registered in the same coordinate the mark
lives in (sNorm ∝ S⁻γ), and a basis mismatch sNorm-vs-price-ratio is exactly a γ−1 gauge defect.
**Why it matters to PH/solvency.** This is a genuine consistency/coherence theorem at the H↔price seam:
PH-1's H is read against the strike, and a mis-registered strike injects a γ-dependent error into where
intrinsic begins — a spurious seam in the storage function. It is the discrete analogue of "the
Hamiltonian must be expressed in a single, consistent set of coordinates." It also underwrites PH-5
(the smooth-pasting boundary is only well-defined once θ is the right coordinate).
**Call: QUEUE as a new obligation (PH-1b or PH-5 prerequisite).** Autonomous formalization (the locked
v26c registration). Recommended target: `crossover_at_K`: with `θ=sNorm(K)`, the mark's OTM→ITM switch
point in price space equals K independent of γ; and a `mixed_basis` negative control. Not heavy.

### (b) Second-wing smooth-pasting mirror — NEW, queue-worthy (extends PH-5)
**Claim.** The American smooth-pasting has a mirror branch the live seam gate already enforces but PH-5
omits: branch B (put / `θ/sNorm`), intrinsic `1 − K/S`, free boundary `S* = K(γ+1)/γ > K`,
`sNorm* = θ·(γ/(γ+1))^γ` — C¹ (value+slope) at S*, symmetric to branch A's `S*=Kγ/(γ+1)<K`.
**Why it matters.** PH-5 as written formalizes ONE branch; the engine has a two-branch C¹ seam (call
boundary below K, put boundary above K). For the PH energy balance to be well-defined across BOTH
ITM/OTM boundaries (no spurious power injection on either wing), both must be C¹. The seam gate also
encodes a directional binding (boundary side keyed to price-space S, so a wing-tag swap fails) that has
real content: the two boundaries are reflections across K.
**Call: QUEUE — fold into PH-5 as branch B (make PH-5 a two-branch obligation).** Autonomous (locked
closed form). ESCALATE only if a branch proves NOT C¹ (settlement-semantics — default: both ARE C¹).

### (c) Price-coordinate-vs-slope as a geometric object — queue-worthy, small
**Claim.** `getMP_raw = P·e^u` is the **carry price coordinate** (equals the oracle at equilibrium),
NOT the reserve slope. The geometric slope is `mpGeom = getMP_raw·e^(−ghMu)`, and
`getMP_raw/|dy/dx| = e^μ = e^ghMu` **exactly** (= dY/du ÷ −dX/du = P·e^(u−μ)). The factor is
11.68/44.52/748.6/13780 at γ=1.5/2/3/4.
**Why it matters.** This is the conflation that caused the slippage bug, and it is a real geometric
distinction PH-2/PH-3 quietly depend on: J's power routing and R's slope-deviation form are stated in
the SLOPE variable, but the engine exposes the PRICE COORDINATE. Any PH obligation that says "slope"
must use `mpGeom`, not `getMP_raw`. A one-line Lean lemma `mpGeom = getMP_raw·e^(−ghMu)` /
`getMP_raw/slope = e^μ` would pin the coordinate map and prevent the same conflation re-entering the
formal layer (every prior gate that conflated them passed — self-consistency does not catch it).
**Call: QUEUE as a small pin (prerequisite lemma for PH-2/PH-3 GH instances).** Autonomous, trivial.

### (d) Directional-consistency invariant — queue-worthy as an R-sign / orientation lemma
**Claim.** Per wing, `sign(K − oracle) == sign(funding wing ±2) == sign(d mark / d sNorm)` (measured in
the curve's OWN coordinate; opposite to d/d-spot due to the reciprocal mark). v26c verifies: CALL all
+, PUT all − (flip across wings).
**Why it matters.** This is the economic-direction invariant — every prior gate tested self-consistency
and never direction, which is exactly how the registration bug survived. In PH terms it is an
**orientation/sign condition coupling the funding port sign (the ±2 wing stamp on the signed gap), the
strike side, and the monotonicity of the mark** — it constrains the SIGN of the port flow relative to
the storage gradient, which is the kind of structural fact that R⪰0 (PH-3) and skew-J (PH-2) live
alongside. Not currently anywhere in the obligation set.
**Call: QUEUE as a directional/orientation lemma (companion to PH-3).** Autonomous. NOTE the funding
sign caveat: a θ-swap into funding FLIPS its sign (manager-tested) — the formalization must keep
funding on the price-measure basis, NOT sNorm. Flag-worthy precisely because it's sign-sensitive.

### (e) Basis-independence of % slippage (e^μ cancels) — minor, optional
**Claim.** `%`-basis slippage is independent of the e^μ basis because `mpGeom = getMP_raw·e^(−ghMu)`
and the `e^μ` (= `e^ghMu`) cancels in the ratio; only the `$` basis (Layer-1 reserve-USD) carries it.
**Why it matters.** A clean invariance fact: the dimensionless (fractional) port quantities are
gauge-invariant under the price-coordinate-vs-slope rescaling, while the dimensionful ones are not — a
mini-version of (c)/(a) at the slippage seam. Theoretically tidy; low solvency stakes.
**Call: NOTE only — fold as a corollary of (c) if (c) is formalized; not its own obligation.**
