### Call wing derivation (intended; re-derived by me)
Unknowns `(a, S)` with `a>0`, `S>0`. System:
  (value) `a·S^(−γ) = 1 − S/K`
  (slope) `−γ·a·S^(−γ−1) = −1/K`   (i.e. `deriv (a·S^(−γ)) = −1/K`)
From (slope): `a·S^(−γ) = S/(γK)` (multiply by `−S/γ`). Substitute into (value):
  `S/(γK) = 1 − S/K`  ⇒  `S·(γ+1) = γK`  ⇒  **`S = Kγ/(γ+1)`** (unique).
Back-substitute: **`a = K^γ·(γ/(γ+1))^γ/(γ+1)`** (= R1's `coeffA`), and the common value is `1/(γ+1)`.

### Put wing derivation
Unknowns `(b, S)` with `b>0`, `S>0`. Intrinsic `1 − K/S`, continuation `b·S^γ`. System:
  (value) `b·S^γ = 1 − K/S`
  (slope) `γ·b·S^(γ−1) = K/S²`   (`deriv (b·S^γ) = γ²/(K(γ+1)²)` is the matched slope value)
From (slope): `b·S^γ = K/(γS)`. Substitute into (value): `K/(γS) = 1 − K/S` ⇒ `K(γ+1)/(γS)=1`
  ⇒ **`S = K(γ+1)/γ`** (unique). Back-substitute: **`b = K^(−γ)·(γ/(γ+1))^γ/(γ+1)`** (= R1's `coeffB`).

`γ > 1`, `K > 0`.
